Frequently Asked Questions

Can I extend my rental?

Yes! Just log in to your dashboard, go to My Bookings, and click "Modify" on your active booking. Extensions are subject to availability.

What happens if I return the car late?

Late returns are charged at the daily rate plus a small late fee. Contact us before your return time if you need to extend.

Is fuel included in the price?

Cars are provided with a full tank and should be returned full. If not, refueling charges apply at market rate plus a service fee.

What documents do I need for pickup?

A valid driver's license, a credit card in your name, and your booking confirmation. International drivers may need an International Driving Permit.
