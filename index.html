Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about renting, driving, fuel, and security deposits.

All you need is a valid original Driving License (held for at least 1 year) and a government-issued photo ID proof (Passport, National ID, or Voter ID). International travelers can rent with an International Driving Permit (IDP) alongside their domestic license.
We offer two transparent models: "Without Fuel" (you receive the vehicle with a full tank and return it full) or "With Fuel Included" (where fuel costs are factored into a pre-paid mileage tier). You can choose whichever fits your road trip budget.
We offer a zero-deposit option for verified credit card holders. For standard bookings, a minimal security deposit is temporarily held and released back to your original payment method within 24 hours of vehicle return, following a quick digital inspection.
Yes! All SelfDrive vehicles carry all-state commercial tourist permits. You are free to drive anywhere within the country. Any applicable state border entry tolls or highway taxes can be paid seamlessly via the vehicle's electronic FASTag / toll pass.
Every rental includes 24/7 Roadside Assistance. In the rare event of mechanical trouble or a puncture, simply tap the SOS button in the app or call our emergency hotline. We will dispatch a mobile support van or arrange an immediate replacement car.
