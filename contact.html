Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to the most common questions about our service, bookings, and policies.

What do I need to rent a car?
You'll need a valid driver's license, a major credit card in your name, and to be at least 21 years old (25 for luxury vehicles). International customers may also need an International Driving Permit.
Is insurance included in the rental price?
Yes. Every rental comes with comprehensive insurance coverage including collision damage waiver, theft protection, and third-party liability. There are no hidden insurance fees.
Can I cancel or modify my booking?
Absolutely. You can cancel or modify your booking free of charge up to 24 hours before your scheduled pickup time. Just log into your account or contact our support team.
What happens if the car breaks down?
Every rental includes 24/7 roadside assistance. If your car becomes undrivable, we'll send help immediately and provide a replacement vehicle if needed — at no extra cost.
Can I add an additional driver?
Yes, additional drivers can be added for $12 per day. The additional driver must meet the same age and license requirements and be present at pickup to sign the rental agreement.
Do you offer one-way rentals?
Yes, one-way rentals are available between most of our locations. A small drop-off fee may apply depending on the distance between pickup and drop-off points. Contact us for details.
